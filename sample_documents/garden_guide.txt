The campus garden team waters the greenhouse basil every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday morning.
Outdoor rosemary beds are watered only on Tuesdays unless the temperature rises above 95 degrees Fahrenheit.

The tool shed combination is 24-18-30. The combination should be changed whenever a volunteer leaves the program.

Compost pickup happens on the first Thursday of each month. Volunteers should place sealed compost buckets beside the north gate before 9 AM.
